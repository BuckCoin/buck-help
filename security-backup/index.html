Security & backup

Keep sensitive wallet information private.

Protect your wallet information

Keep seed phrases, private keys, passwords, and wallet backup files private. Never post them in a public discussion or send them to someone offering help.

Keep secrets out of support requests

This Help Center will never ask you to provide seed phrases, private keys, passwords, or wallet backup files.

Backup and recovery guidance

Detailed BUCK Wallet backup and recovery guidance is being prepared. Backup formats, storage locations, and recovery methods are not documented here yet.

Avoid deleting or replacing wallet files while you are unsure of their purpose. Do not follow an unverified recovery procedure.
